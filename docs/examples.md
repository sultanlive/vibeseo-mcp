# Example prompts

Ask VibeSEO MCP the way you'd ask an SEO teammate — it calls the right tools and returns concrete next steps. A few starting points.

---

## Keyword research

> "Find low-difficulty comparison keywords for a bootstrapped invoicing app."

> "Find comparison keywords for my invoicing app that are realistic to rank for."

VibeSEO returns a prioritized topic list, e.g.
- `freelance invoice software vs spreadsheet` — commercial intent
- `best invoice app for consultants` — lower difficulty
- `invoice generator alternatives` — comparison angle

---

## Domain analysis

> "Summarize the pages driving organic traffic for this competitor."

> "Show me the top 20 keywords this domain ranks for, with positions and search volume."

---

## Backlinks

> "Compare backlink velocity for these three competitors."

> "List the top referring domains for `example.com` over the last 90 days."

---

## Site audits

> "Audit my project and give me the five fixes to ship first."

> "Audit my project and tell me what to fix before publishing more articles."

VibeSEO turns crawl data into next actions, e.g.
- Fix missing canonicals on 6 pages
- Compress oversized hero images
- Add internal links from service pages to new articles

---

## GSC performance

> "Which articles gained impressions but still have weak CTR?"

> "Show me query-level diagnostics for the page that lost the most traffic this month."

---

## Content workflow

> "Generate the next scheduled draft and prepare it for review."

> "Show ready posts, generate the next draft, and schedule the strongest one."

VibeSEO manages the content workflow, e.g.
- 2 Ready posts waiting for approval
- 1 Idea moved into Drafting
- Next approved article scheduled for Friday

---

## Reminder: the approval gate stays

MCP can help manage the workflow, but VibeSEO keeps the approval gate before content goes live. The assistant can move ideas into drafts, generate copy, and propose schedules — but a human still approves inside VibeSEO before anything publishes.
